About Lauren

Originally from Cincinnati, Ohio, I am currently a junior at Miami University in Oxford. Over the past 4 years, my passion for visual storytelling has grown from a high school hobby into a full service creative practice.

Whether I'm photographing seniors on Miami's historic red-brick campus, candid events around Cincinnati, or sporting events, my aim is always the same: to create warm, timeless imagery that reflects real emotion.
